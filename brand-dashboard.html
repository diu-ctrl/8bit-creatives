Brand Partner Guide
Plain-English documentation on how brand partner dashboards and approvals function.
Section 1 — Reviewing and approving deliverables
When deliverables reach "In Brand Review", your brand manager can watch the draft directly via the Google Drive vault link. You can leave comments or request revisions directly with your designated 8Bit account executive.
Section 2 — Understanding telemetry and ROI
Performance metrics aggregate view counts, average watch duration, click-through rates on description track links, and social mentions. Reports are certified and updated every 24 hours.
