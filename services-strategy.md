# Services.html — Complete Rebuild Strategy & Antigravity Prompt

The current services.html is **3966px tall** with only 5 content sections:
1. Hero — "Campaigns, consulting & activations that reach India's 500M+ gamers" + "Discover" link
2. The Approach — 3-step process (placeholder titles, generic)

**Problems:**
- The page is extremely THIN for a "Services" page — a premium agency's services page is usually 6000-8000px tall with detailed service breakdowns
- The hero mentions "campaigns, consulting & activations" but never explains WHAT services 8Bit actually offers
- The 3-step "Approach" has generic placeholder titles
- The case studies and logo strip are DUPLICATED from the landing page — no unique content
- There's no service breakdown, no deliverables, no methodology, no capabilities, no testimonials, no FAQ
- The page doesn't tell a brand "here's what we can do for you" — it just has a vague hero + duplicated content

## Research — What Premium Agency Services Pages Do

### Pentagram (pentagram.com/work)
- Each service (Identity, Digital, Products, etc.) gets its own filterable category
- Each project links to a deep case study
- The services page IS the work page — services are shown through the work, not described

### Instrument (instrument.com)
- Services page has: a bold hero statement → a short manifesto paragraph → a grid of "what we do" cards (Strategy, Design, Engineering, Production) → selected work → CTA
- Each service card has: title, one-line description, and 3-4 sub-deliverables as small tags
- The page feels like a magazine table of contents — clean, scannable, editorial

### Locomotive (locomotive.ca)
- Services page opens with a large manifesto statement
- Then a grid of service blocks, each with: number, title, paragraph description, and tags for deliverables
- Each service block is clickable → expands into a detailed view with process, deliverables, and related work

### BASIC/DEPT (basicagency.com)
- Services are organized by "For Brands" / "For Creators" / "For Platforms" (same as 8Bit's landing page!)
- Each section has: a title, a paragraph, a list of specific deliverables, and a "See work" link to filtered case studies
- The page is text-heavy with editorial typography — minimal imagery, maximum information

### MediaMonks (mediamonks.com)
- Services are filterable by category (Creative, Production, Technology, Strategy)
- Each service has a one-line description and a "Learn more" link
- The page is a clean filterable grid — no long paragraphs

### Mother London (motherlondon.com)
- The services page is basically a manifesto — a long-form essay about how the agency thinks, with the services woven into the narrative
- Very editorial, very text-heavy, very on-brand

### Synthesis — what 8Bit's services page should be

A hybrid approach combining:
1. **BASIC/DEPT's "For Brands / For Creators / For Platforms" structure** (matches the landing page)
2. **Instrument's editorial service cards** (title + one-line description + sub-deliverables as tags)
3. **Locomotive's numbered service blocks** (clean, scannable, editorial)
4. **A unique section 8Bit needs that others don't have: the "Gaming-Native" positioning** — why a gaming-native agency is different from a generic influencer agency

The page should feel like a **magazine feature about 8Bit's capabilities** — text-heavy, editorial, informative, with clear service breakdowns and real deliverables.

## Design Guidelines

- **Background:** Pure black (#000000) for the hero → smooth black-to-white gradient transition before the footer (same as work.html's gradient).
- **Fonts:** Geist Sans only. Weight 300 for body text, 400 for section titles, 500 for sub-headings. No Geist Mono. No italic body text.
- **Colors:** White text on dark background. Muted white (70% opacity) for secondary text. No orange text anywhere (per anti-list). The only color accent is the white-on-black contrast.
- **Layout:** Left-aligned content within a 1400px max-width container (matching the rest of the site). Generous vertical spacing between sections (80-120px). Editorial, not dashboard-like.
- **Service blocks:** Numbered (01, 02, 03), with the number in Geist 400, 14px, muted white. Title in Geist 400, 32px. Description in Geist 300, 18px. Deliverables as small tags (capsule pills, 12px, white-15% border, white text).
- **No eyebrow titles** above section headers. No all-caps text. No squarish corners (14px minimum on any block element). No hover-to-popup. No hover-to-zoom.
- **Responsive:** Tablet (768-1279px) and mobile (≤767px) breakpoints. Service blocks stack vertically on mobile. Text scales down.
- **Motion:** Subtle scroll-reveal (fade + translateY, 400ms ease-out) on section content. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
