Creator Guide
Simple instructions on how to use your creator portal and deliver on campaigns.
Section 1 — How campaigns are assigned
Whenever an 8Bit ops account manager closes a brand deal tailored for your channel, it appears immediately on your dashboard under My Campaigns. You will also receive an alert on your dedicated Slack channel with the brief and SOW.
Section 2 — Uploading deliverables
Upload your rough cuts and draft videos directly to your campaign folder in Google Drive using the provided links. Once uploaded, update the status dropdown on this dashboard to "In Review" so the ops team is notified to review.
Section 3 — Payment timelines
Payments are released automatically once deliverables have been signed off by the brand partner and the corresponding milestone invoice clears. You can view all incoming payments and cleared transactions in My Payments.
