04 - Information we collect & how it is used

Directly from you through forms:

When you use the interactive demonstration contact forms on this website (such as the campaign inquiry form or creator representation form), the information you provide may include:

  • •Your name
  • •Your email address
  • •Company, brand, or agency name
  • •The text message, campaign details, or portfolio feedback you submit

This information is processed through Formspree (a secure form delivery service) and delivered directly to Divyaraj Chauhan for communication and project feedback. It is never sold, leased, or shared with third-party marketing brokers.

Technical & performance metrics:

To ensure this website operates smoothly across devices, we use privacy-friendly edge telemetry through Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights. This collects anonymous, aggregate technical data such as browser type, operating system, general country/region, and Core Web Vitals (LCP, CLS, TTFB). These metrics do not collect personal identifiers, do not use invasive tracking cookies, and do not track you across other websites.

05 - Cookies & client-side storage

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. We do not use third-party advertising tracking pixels (such as Facebook Pixel or Google Ads Remarketing).

Minimal browser session storage and local storage are utilized strictly for UI and user experience preferences, such as remembering navigation sections during scrolling, maintaining preloader animation states, and preserving form field progress locally in your browser.
