Guided by our values in every step

We don't just manage creators; we build careers, protect culture, and bridge the gap between gaming and the brands that want to reach it. These are the principles that govern every decision we make.

Transparency

Every number, every metric, every contract term — shared openly with the brands and creators we work with. No hidden clauses, no vanity metrics, no inflated reach. Just the raw data that proves the work.

Quality

From the first campaign brief to the final broadcast feed, we hold the bar high. We don't do filler content, we don't do generic streams, and we don't do shortcuts. If it doesn't elevate the creator or the brand, it doesn't ship.

Passion

We're gamers first, agency second. We've been in the lobbies, we've watched the streams, and we understand the culture because we are part of it. That intrinsic understanding is the edge we bring to every partnership.

OUR STORY

We are India's first gaming-native talent agency. Our services span the full creator economy — from talent management and brand partnerships to live broadcast production and platform strategy.

In 2018, Thug, Goldy, and Mortal realized that Indian gaming creators were signing deals without anyone actually understanding their craft. We started 8Bit to change that. We built an agency that speaks the language of the lobby, the stream, and the audience, while holding the professional standards of a top-tier media firm.

Today, we've built the infrastructure between gaming culture and brand budgets. From executing Kick's first India partnership to managing the Global Chess League broadcast desk, every campaign we ship is measured, reported, and optimized against real outcomes — not vanity metrics.

Through the S8UL ecosystem — uniting 8Bit Creatives, S8UL Esports, and the 15,000 sq. ft. Gaming House 2.0 in Mumbai — we provide our creators with world-class production facilities, legal advocacy, and commercial infrastructure. We don't just find brand deals; we build long-term, sustainable careers for the people shaping Indian gaming.

We work with brands entering gaming for the first time, creators building long-term careers, and platforms seeking authentic audience distribution in India. If you're looking to understand the room, we're the people who can show you in.
