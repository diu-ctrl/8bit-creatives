Guide
Everything you need to know about the ops portal. Plain English, no jargon.
Section 1 — Dashboard
This is your home base. It shows active campaigns, available creators, pending approvals, and deliverables due this week. Use it to get a quick snapshot of what's happening across all campaigns.
Section 2 — Campaign Pipeline
This is your Kanban board. Campaigns move left to right: Brief Intake → In Production → Pending Approval → Delivered → Live & Reporting. Click any card to see full details, including the brief, assigned creators, deliverables checklist, SOW, and next steps.
Section 3 — Creator Roster
This is your creator list. Each card shows the creator's handle, real name, current status (Available, Booked, In Production), and subscriber count. Click a creator to see their current campaigns, deliverables count, payment status, contract status, and performance metrics.
Section 4 — Brief Intake
Use this form to submit a new brand brief. Fill in the brand name, campaign title, category, timeline, desired creators, deliverable formats, and a Google Drive link to the brief document. The system auto-creates a campaign record and notifies the ops team.
Section 5 — Approvals Queue
Deliverables that need review appear here. Internal review means the 8Bit ops team needs to approve it first. Brand approval means the brand needs to sign off. Once both approve, the deliverable moves to Delivered.
Section 6 — File Vault
All deliverable files are stored here, linked to Google Drive. Each file shows the campaign, creator, version, upload date, and status. Upload new versions, download approved files, and track version history.
Section 7 — Reports
Post-campaign metrics and performance reports. Download PDF reports for client presentations.
Section 8 — Creator Dashboard
If you're a creator, this is your personal dashboard (separate file: creator-dashboard.html). See your active campaigns, deliverables checklist, payment status, contract, and performance metrics.
Section 9 — Brand Dashboard
If you're a brand partner, this is your campaign overview (separate file: brand-dashboard.html). See your campaigns, performance metrics, deliverables status, assigned creators, budget tracking, and post-campaign reports.
Section 10 — Platform Dashboard
If you're a platform partner (separate file: platform-dashboard.html), this shows your talent migration status, content performance on your platform, growth metrics, and contract status.
Section 11 — Settings
Configure your account, notification preferences, and integrations.
Section 12 — Permissions
Manage who has access to what. Admins can see everything. Creators see only their own data. Brands see only their campaigns. Platforms see only their migration data.
